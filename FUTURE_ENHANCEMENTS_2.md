## 📋 Executive Summary

This document represents the second major iteration of ARTemis enhancement planning, compiled through comprehensive research of the top 200 features from leading professional digital art and photo manipulation software including Adobe Photoshop, Procreate, Clip Studio Paint, Krita, Affinity Photo, Corel Painter, Rebelle, and ArtRage.

The features identified here represent cutting-edge capabilities in the digital art industry as of 2024-2025, with particular focus on:
- AI-powered creative tools
- Advanced natural media simulation
- Professional workflow optimization
- Collaborative and cloud features
- Performance and large-canvas capabilities
