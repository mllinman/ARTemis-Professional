# Quick Start Guide - ARTemis Subscription Service

## 🚀 Getting Started in 3 Minutes

### Step 1: Access ARTemis
Open `src/login.html` in your web browser.

### Step 2: Choose Your Login Method

#### Option A: Guest Mode (Fastest)
1. Click **"Continue as Guest (Free Tier)"**
2. Start creating immediately with free features

#### Option B: Google Sign-In (Recommended)
1. Click **"Continue with Google"**
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Get free tier access instantly

#### Option C: Admin Login (Full Access)
1. Click the **"Admin"** tab
2. Enter password: `admin123`
3. Get instant Pro access

### Step 3: Start Creating!
You're now in ARTemis and ready to paint!

## 🔄 Upgrading to Pro

### From Within the App:
1. Click your **user avatar** (top-right corner)
2. Click **"Upgrade to Pro"**
3. Review the pricing and features
4. Click **"Upgrade Now"**
5. ✨ All features unlock instantly!

### Or Click Any Locked Tool:
- Tools marked with **"PRO"** badge
- Clicking shows upgrade prompt
- Quick path to subscription modal

## 💡 Tips & Tricks

### Trying Pro Features
- Click on any tool with a **PRO** badge
- See exactly what you're getting
- Easy upgrade process

### Managing Your Subscription
1. Click your **user avatar**
2. Select **"Manage Subscription"**
3. View current plan or cancel

### Switching Accounts
1. Click your **user avatar**
2. Click **"Sign Out"**
3. Return to login screen
4. Choose different auth method

## ❓ FAQ

### Q: Do I need to pay to use ARTemis?
**A:** No! Basic tier is completely free with essential features.

### Q: How do I access Pro features?
**A:** Either upgrade to Pro ($5/month) or use Admin login (password: `admin123`).

### Q: Is my work saved?
**A:** Yes! Your work is saved locally in your browser. Use File → Save to create project files.

### Q: Can I cancel anytime?
**A:** Yes! Cancel anytime from "Manage Subscription" in the user menu.

### Q: What payment methods are accepted?
**A:** In demo mode, no payment needed. Production version will use Stripe (credit card, debit card).

### Q: Will my subscription auto-renew?
**A:** Yes, monthly subscriptions auto-renew unless canceled.
