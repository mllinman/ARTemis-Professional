# BulletDrop Studios LLC - Business Plan Summary

**Quick Reference Guide**

## Executive Overview

BulletDrop Studios LLC offers **ARTemis**, a professional browser-based digital painting application that competes with Adobe Photoshop, Corel Painter, Krita, and Procreate at a fraction of the cost.

### Key Differentiators
- ✅ **Zero Installation**: Works in any browser, any device
- ✅ **Affordable**: $5/month Pro vs. $50+ for competitors  
- ✅ **Professional Features**: 158+ brush presets, unlimited layers
- ✅ **Cross-Platform**: Windows, Mac, Linux, iOS, Android, ChromeOS

---

## Business Model

### Product Tiers

| Tier | Price | Target Users | Key Features |
|------|-------|--------------|--------------|
| **Free** | $0/month | Hobbyists, students | 5 layers, 10 brushes, basic tools |
| **Pro** | $5/month | Professional artists | Unlimited layers, 158+ brushes, all tools |
| **Team** | $12/user/month | Studios, schools | Shared libraries, collaboration |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Large orgs | SSO, custom deployment, SLA |

### Revenue Streams
1. **Primary**: Pro subscriptions ($5/month)
2. **Secondary**: Team/Enterprise licenses
3. **Future**: Brush marketplace, plugin marketplace

## Risk Mitigation

### Key Risks & Solutions

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Competitive Response | First-mover advantage, strong community |
| Low Conversion | A/B testing, trial periods, value demonstration |
| Browser Limitations | WebGL acceleration, Electron fallback |
| High Churn | Product excellence, regular updates, community |
| Payment Issues | Redundant processors, retry logic |
