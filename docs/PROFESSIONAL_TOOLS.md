## Blueline Pro 11" × 17" Artboard

A professional comic book and illustration artboard preset that includes:

### Features
- **Dimensions:** 3300 × 5100 pixels (11" × 17" at 300 DPI)
- **Blueline Layer:** Non-photo blue guidelines automatically created
- **Trim Margins:** 0.5" (150px) margins for safe area
- **Center Guides:** Horizontal and vertical center lines
- **Registration Marks:** Corner registration marks for alignment
- **Opacity:** 60% for the blueline layer (easy to see but won't interfere)

### Usage
1. Go to **File → New with Size** (Ctrl+Shift+N)
2. Click on **"Blueline Pro 11×17" (300dpi)"** preset button
3. Click **Create**

The canvas will be created with a blueline layer containing professional trim marks and guides. You can:
- Draw on new layers above the blueline
- Turn off the blueline layer visibility when exporting final art
- Use the guides for panel layout and composition

The blueline color (non-photo blue) traditionally doesn't appear when scanning black and white artwork, making it perfect for comic book and illustration work.

## Keyboard Shortcut Customization

ARTemis now features a complete keyboard shortcut customization system, allowing you to tailor the application to your workflow.

### Accessing Shortcut Customization
1. Open **File → Settings** (Ctrl+,)
2. Scroll to the **Keyboard Shortcuts** section
3. Click **"Customize Keyboard Shortcuts"**

### How to Customize
1. In the customization dialog, click on any shortcut you want to change
2. The shortcut field will highlight and show "Press a key..."
3. Press your desired key
4. The shortcut will update immediately
5. Click **"Save Changes"** to apply

### Features
- **Real-time validation:** The system prevents duplicate shortcuts
- **Visual feedback:** Active shortcuts are highlighted during editing
- **Persistent storage:** Shortcuts are saved to browser localStorage
- **Reset option:** Restore all shortcuts to defaults with one click

## Oil Paint Workflow Recommendations

While ARTemis's current brush engine provides excellent results, here are tips for achieving realistic oil painting effects:

### Layering
1. Start with thin layers (low opacity/flow)
2. Build up gradually with multiple passes
3. Use the Oil Impasto Heavy brush for final textured highlights

### Blending
1. Use a soft brush with low opacity for smooth transitions
2. The "Oil Glaze" brush (from the Oil Paint category) works well for glazing
3. Adjust flow to control paint buildup

### Texture Effects
1. Use angle jitter to vary brush orientation
2. Apply scatter for more organic brush strokes
3. Increase spacing for visible brush dabs

## Tips for Professional Use

1. **2B Graphite Pencil:** Use with light pressure for sketching, increase pressure for darker lines
2. **Nib Pens:** The high smoothing prevents jittery lines - perfect for clean comic inking
3. **Paasche Airbrush:** Build up gradually with multiple passes for smooth gradients
4. **W&N Brushes:** Take advantage of pressure sensitivity for natural brush strokes
5. **Blueline Artboard:** Keep the blueline layer at the bottom of your layer stack
6. **Keyboard Shortcuts:** Customize to match your most-used applications (Photoshop, Krita, etc.)
