Everything You Need to Create

🖌️ Advanced Brush Engine

Professional brush system with full pressure sensitivity, flow control, velocity dynamics, and tilt support. Create natural, expressive strokes with 172+ presets across 17 categories.

  • Adjustable size, opacity, hardness, and flow
  • 3 smoothing modes for perfect control
  • Rotation, scatter, and texture options
  • Natural Media, Metallic, and Special Effects
🖌️

📚 Unlimited Layers

Organize your artwork with unlimited layers (Pro). Paint, Vector, Filter, and Group layer types. Full layer management with blending modes, opacity control, and more.

  • Move, duplicate, merge, and flatten layers
  • 15+ blend modes including advanced color modes
  • Layer thumbnails and visual organization
  • Non-destructive adjustment layers
📚

🖼️ Photo-to-Paint System

Transform photos into artwork with 7 artistic styles: Oil Paint, Acrylic, Watercolor, Comic Book, Cartoon, Anime, and Concept Art. Real-time adjustable parameters for complete creative control.

  • Professional edge detection algorithms
  • Real-time preview with adjustable sliders
  • Authentic artistic style simulation
  • Perfect for illustration and concept work
🖼️

🎨 Professional Color System

Advanced color tools including professional color wheel with HSV triangle, wet palette mixing, and screen-wide eyedropper. Create and blend colors naturally.

  • Wet palette with wetness and bleeding controls
  • Real-time color mixing as you paint
  • Sample colors from anywhere on screen
  • Quick color swatches and history
