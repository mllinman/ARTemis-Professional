Frequently Asked Questions

Does ARTemis work on my device?

Yes! ARTemis works on any device with a modern web browser: Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android, and ChromeOS. No installation required.

Do I need a stylus or drawing tablet?

Not required! ARTemis works with mouse, touchpad, touch screens, and stylus/tablets. Full pressure sensitivity is supported with Wacom, XP-Pen, Huion, and other tablets.

Can I export my work to use in Photoshop or other software?

Absolutely! Export to PSD (with all layers preserved), TIFF, PNG, JPEG, or WebP. Your layers, blend modes, and opacity settings are maintained in PSD exports.

What's the difference between Basic and Pro?

Basic is free with 5 layers and 10 brushes - perfect for getting started. Pro ($5/month) unlocks unlimited layers, 178+ brushes, all advanced tools, professional filters, and export features.

Is my work saved automatically?

You can save your projects locally using File → Save Project (Ctrl+S). Pro users get cloud sync for automatic backup and access across devices.

Can I cancel my Pro subscription anytime?

Yes! No annual commitment required. Cancel anytime and keep access until the end of your billing period.
