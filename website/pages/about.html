About BulletDrop Studios

Creating professional digital art tools that democratize creativity

Our Mission

To democratize professional digital art creation by providing accessible, affordable, and powerful creative tools that rival industry-leading software while maintaining zero barriers to entry.

Our Story

BulletDrop Studios was founded with a simple vision: professional-grade creative tools shouldn't be locked behind expensive subscriptions or complicated installations.

We saw artists struggling with expensive software, limited device compatibility, and complex installation processes. So we set out to build something different - a professional digital painting application that works directly in your browser, on any device, with zero installation required.

ARTemis is our flagship product, built from the ground up with modern web technologies. It delivers 158+ professional brush presets, unlimited layers, advanced tools, and features that rival Adobe Photoshop, Corel Painter, and Procreate - all for a fraction of the cost, or completely free.

Today, thousands of artists worldwide use ARTemis to create stunning digital artwork, from concept art and illustrations to comics and photo editing. And we're just getting started.
