Frequently Asked Questions

Can I try Pro features before subscribing?

Yes! You can start with the free Basic tier and upgrade to Pro anytime. We recommend starting free to get familiar with the app.

How does billing work?

Pro subscription is billed monthly at $5/month. You can cancel anytime and your subscription will remain active until the end of your billing period.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and PayPal through our secure payment processor.

Can I switch between plans?

Absolutely! You can upgrade from Basic to Pro instantly. If you downgrade from Pro to Basic, your subscription will remain active until the end of the billing period.

What happens to my files if I cancel Pro?

Your files remain accessible. However, projects with more than 5 layers will need to be merged or flattened to continue editing on the Basic plan.

Do you offer student or educational discounts?

We're working on educational pricing. For now, the free Basic tier is perfect for students learning digital art.

Is there a free trial for Pro?

The Basic tier serves as an extended trial. It's completely free and gives you access to core features so you can evaluate ARTemis before upgrading.
